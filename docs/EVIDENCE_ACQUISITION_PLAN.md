# Evidence acquisition plan

Phase 2C · NQ Quant Research Lab · Base `f2ef963` · 2026-11-15

The minimum package for the original protocol is a dated, complete eligible NQ outright book and valid preceding-session volume comparison at each approved origin, plus independently admissible calendar, bar availability and sample governance. Evidence may come from retained files or new acquisition. Recover the protocol and identify the required availability clock before paying to fill gaps.

## Minimum evidence per admitted date

1. **Roster and state.** A complete pre-origin CME NQ outright snapshot and all updates through the cutoff, or a venue-certified equivalent, with dated symbols/IDs, activation, last-trade time and expiry. Authenticate the approved initialization and monotonic expiry state. A short independent universe check must be able to find instruments even if they never printed.
2. **Volume and cutoff.** The approved electronic-volume metric for the scheduled preceding CME trade date, across all its active segments and for every selectable candidate. An exact total or a contemporaneous, same-metric bound strong enough to eliminate a competitor under the approved tie rule is sufficient for the selector if authorized. If complete totals had yet to become available, keep the date unresolved under the applicable exclusion policy. Require affirmative scope and continuity for a zero. Separate reset, recovery, late reports and corrections from regular updates.
3. **Calendar and measurement.** Dated NQ-specific schedule evidence, actual status and outage/gap reconciliation, independent cash-session evidence, and sufficient observed raw outright bars for any admitted date and required predecessor. Timestamp and version each required input on the clock the protocol specifies. A documented bound can substitute only if that protocol accepts it. Keep the selected instrument fixed when target coverage later fails.
4. **Authority and sample.** The authenticated EXP-001 protocol and amendment register, independently approved rules and exceptions, frozen exposure-adjusted data-only eligibility, duration/power and split verification, and independent custody. These remain certification gates even if the acquisition is technically successful. ## Order of work and economics

1. **Resolve what can be bought.** Recover the protocol and check original collectors for exact clocks. Inventory surviving files and calendars, then obtain written schema, clock, coverage and revision answers from candidate providers. Request a coverage statement for quiet contracts, resets and unknown channel packets. A product listing establishes that the product exists. Exact date, channel, message and entitlement coverage still need confirmation.
2. **Price the bounded request.** Compare normalized schema/date-range estimates using Databento `Historical.metadata.get_cost` for the exact requests and account terms, a CME DataMine exact-scope quote, and raw PCAP minimum order, bytes/storage, decoder and license terms. Check whether parties can review the evidence under the license. Record cash cost, staff-days and total landed cost separately. Historical API estimates can vary with query boundaries and actual bytes. Current terms and commitments must come from the quote. [Databento historical cost API](https://databento.com/docs/api-historical) · [Databento pricing](https://databento.com/pricing) · [CME DataMine](https://datamine.new.cmegroup.com/)
3. **Run the approved pilot only if the evidence exists at a viable cost.** Agree a cash cap and hands-on budget before retrieval and keep an immutable request manifest outside Git and away from sealed releases. The overlapping work estimates imply roughly **2–4 person-days for authority, source and quote triage**, then **5–10 person-days for acquisition, decoding and independent pilot review** if the sources are supplied. Lead time and fees are unknown. A separate budget decision is needed for even one commercial order. [Pilot specification](MINIMAL_ACQUISITION_PILOT.md)
4. **Estimate expansion on measured volume and exception cost.** Inventory missing trading-date by channel groups to avoid pricing each of the 149 target labels as a separate delivery. Use measured bytes and analyst time per ordinary and exceptional date, plus fixed decoder/review costs. Treat different eras separately. Rough staffing allowance for the audited scope is **2–6 person-weeks after successful pilot**, before any certification, with high uncertainty from channel packaging, exception rates and provider retrieval. A pre-2017 provider-receipt claim would require an alternate contemporaneous source. Full-archive spend and permitted sample size remain unpriced and unproved.

No quote, price, vendor delivery, historical market-data retrieval or pilot run is claimed here. A missing indispensable clock, unverifiable roster/volume metric, or cost above the approved cap makes the original path a **NO-GO for that scope**.
