## Historical alternatives and validation

- **Normalized schemas.** Quote `definition`, `status` and `trades` for exact dates, with schema versions, DBN metadata, raw-symbol and instrument mappings and quality conditions. Filter historical queries by the documented index timestamp, generally `ts_recv` when present. Obtain any fixed boundary padding needed to capture late messages, then enforce a separate availability mask. A metadata range, mapping success, `partial` symbol list or clean dataset-level condition supplies no per-candidate coverage certificate. The Jan 2023 pilot retained partial definition coverage flags and an empty OHLCV response for six symbols. Per the acquisition ledger, trades and status remained unstarted. [Historical metadata and conditions](https://databento.com/docs/api-reference-historical/metadata/metadata-get-dataset-condition)
- **Raw venue packets.** Request Databento PCAP or CME DataMine PCAP/Market Depth for the applicable product channels, including Instrument Replay, incremental, and any necessary recovery and status records. Obtain the dated channel configuration, SBE templates, channel resets, gaps/recovery report, hardware timestamps and coverage statement. Published offerings establish product families. Confirm exact 2022/2023 NQ channel coverage, retention and whether the capture includes the needed message types. An MBO order-event download is also normalized and does not by itself certify underlying packet continuity or carry all statistics. [Databento PCAP](https://databento.com/pcaps) · [CME historical product catalog](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/real-time-and-historical-data.html) · [CME MDP message specification](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457323107/)
- **CME Time & Sales.** Use as a licensed reconciliation candidate. CME's Globex futures report describes insertion, cancel and correction indicators. DataMine advertises a Futures & Options Time & Sales product from Aug 22, 2022. Confirm the NQ-specific legacy SKU or archive for June and earlier August 2022, whether both original and corrective records are retained, whether correction timestamps are original publication times, and treatment of spreads and non-Globex prints. CME's older Market Depth FAQ identifies Time & Sales as post-trade processed. A trade timestamp with corrected quantity can corroborate an eventual record and still leave the cutoff quantity unknown. [DataMine catalog](https://datamine.new.cmegroup.com/catalog?category=FE06) · [CME About Time & Sales](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/about-time-sales.html) · [CME Market Depth FAQ](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/files/cme-group-market-depth-faq.pdf)

When testing raw-event reconstruction, retain event, publisher send, receiver capture, gateway send and local availability separately. Rebuild the state using only records evidenced by the cutoff on the required clock. Record breaks and corrections with both affected event time and first availability. Use later revisions to audit drift. A later correction must leave the earlier state intact. A last-minute bar may end exactly at the modeled cutoff, so require timestamped completion or apply the approved missing-input rule. If only venue or provider capture can be established, label reconstruction at that exact clock. Any substitution for a required downstream clock needs separate protocol approval.
