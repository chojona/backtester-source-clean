## Questions to resolve against the recovered clauses

- For roster changes, volume, calendars/status, bars, feature readiness, order decisions and any modeled fill, record the source clause and amendment, applicable evidence clock, exact payload vintage and governing cutoff. Resolve effective time versus first known time, and scheduled predecessor boundaries versus observed delivery.
- Extract exact UTC cutoff construction, strict or inclusive comparison, same-timestamp ordering and tie handling, accepted clock uncertainty or bounds, and policy for missing or late evidence. A complete interval ending exactly at the origin establishes a boundary. Timestamp precision alone cannot establish usability at that origin.
- Extract version and reset rules for breaks, cancel/correct messages, late arrivals, recovery, and provider reissues. Require linkage between affected input, version and first availability. Determine when a revision can enter a later decision and when uncertainty blocks a comparison. Keep later records outside the earlier information set.
- Identify any expressly permitted bar-construction, proxy, exclusion or delay rule and the claims it supports. Record gaps or conflicts for the research owner and independent reviewer. A1–A6, draft pilot cutoffs and code defaults do not establish EXP-001 policy.

If the required clock is downstream and the relevant original evidence or authorized bound cannot be recovered, close procurement for the affected original-protocol scope before buying trades. A provider or venue clock result can still be documented as evidence about that specific clock. The five-origin comparison and any separately designed exploratory execution require separate approval.
