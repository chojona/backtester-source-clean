## Before opening new data

1. Have the custodian clear these exact evidence windows and field projections without opening the holdout. If a sampled window cannot be cleared, stop and seek a revised, independently approved design. Confirm protocol authority or document that results can only be diagnostic. Freeze metric, roster effective/known tests, allowed clock, UTC cutoff equality, event/receipt filters, correction/recovery handling and unknown/zero rules. Record any unavailable authority as a failed precondition to original-protocol success.
2. Seek affirmative provider confirmation of the narrowest qualifying baseline, delta, status and volume records. For each predecessor allow one complete pre-segment roster baseline and unbroken changes from there to its cutoff, plus the published listing/expiry edge records. Use the dated NQ channel set only. Include both sides of scheduled resets/halts and at most 60 minutes after the cutoff for lateness/revision diagnostics. Any later boundary record is quarantined from the as-of calculation. The listing-edge validation may use definition/status records through the first Sunday open in November 2023 and the June expiry/replenishment reference, with their receipt times preserved. Avoid bulk extra dates. If the opening state cannot be proved in scope, record a failure and price any extension separately.
3. Record date-specific schema and semantic versions, clock accuracy and sync, channel and recovery requirements, and all vendor costs and licensing. Freeze the data manifest, approved cash cap and analyst budget before downloads. Obtain owner authorization for commercial retrieval. Stop if the quoted package omits a necessary evidence class. The order should exclude strategy or cash-target bars and restrict source records to instrument/size/state/timing and correction linkage where practical. Venue packets may contain additional fields. The decoder may retain restricted raw packets under license and project only the evidence fields for review.

## Procedure and pass criteria

| Check | Required result for an original-protocol pilot pass |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline and dates | All five cutoffs and every active segment agree with dated NQ schedules or verified NQ group mapping, and with actual status and continuity. Resets are bound to the correct CME trade date. Track the cash calendar independently. Reject discrepancies that could alter membership or volume. |
| Complete roster | At each cutoff independently reconcile the full CME NQ quarterly outright set against the effective schedule and a complete replay or venue-certified snapshot/delta source, including all zero-print candidates, activation and termination. Resolve every discrepancy with dated evidence. Record preannounced and not-yet-effective listings separately. Align the eligible set with any required monotonic state using an independently evidenced starting state. If state authority or evidence is missing, report venue-roster reconstruction separately and fail the strict eligibility check. |
| Complete predecessor totals | Account for every reconstructed eligible contract and predecessor segment. Each cell has an exact as-of volume or a proven zero. Check sums/state at the approved clock against independently decoded volume/recovery records, an independently decoded authoritative capture, or a timestamped metric-equivalent statement. Reconcile differences exactly, including spread attribution, corrections, resets and recovery. Missing cells, unresolved sequence or group-state gaps, or wrong-metric comparisons fail. A contender bound could satisfy a future selector comparison where approved, but this pilot tests exact recoverability. |
| Causal cutoff | Record acquisition, effective event and required availability times separately. Build one ledger from cutoff-truncated inputs and independently rebuild from the full permitted evidence with an as-of filter. Both must yield identical roster/volume states at each origin. Verify that revisions and definitions after the cutoff have zero effect on the cutoff ledger. Reconcile the final completed-session volume and any records unavailable by cutoff. Later totals can validate eventual volume while leaving the cutoff volume incomplete. |
| Reproducibility and economics | Pin raw files, clock evidence, schedule sources, request parameters and decoder versions using hashes. A second reviewer reproduces all date/candidate classifications and reconciles the quoted and actual data cost, bytes, labor and any license constraints. The work stays within the approved pilot cap. |

For each cell keep the instrument and dated identity, first known and effective times, predecessor segment coverage, status/reset/change references, exact volume-or-unknown classification, as-of evidence pointer and all exclusion reasons. Keep source evidence outside Git in a separate restricted location. A compact aggregate can report each gate as passed, partial or failed. The pilot may compute the comparison needed to prove completeness using candidate volumes; no target data, strategy feature, backtest, performance metric or holdout content is needed.

**Decision rule.** All five origins must pass every check for a limited **GO to price a staged expansion**. A pass is confined to the evidenced clock, venue coverage, metric and dates. Venue/provider-clock reconstruction without the required downstream clock is a technical partial result and an original-protocol **NO-GO**. Any missing clock, unresolved candidate, unknown required segment or budget overrun stops expansion. No result from this pilot is historical admission. [Phase 2C decision](PHASE_2C_DECISION.md)
