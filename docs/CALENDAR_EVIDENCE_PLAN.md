Phase 2C evidence plan · Base `f2ef963` · 2026-11-15

Record both the **scheduled** CME NQ open, pre-open, pause, close and trading-date assignment, and the **actual** group/instrument state from the venue feed. Bind the Nasdaq cash schedule independently for the proposed cash grid. Retain sources, effective date and issuance/version, dates in America/Chicago and America/New_York, and UTC half-open segments. Confirm offsets across daylight-saving changes. Process the scheduled immediate predecessor even if the archive has no bars for it.

## Acceptance record

For each date and instrument preserve the cited schedule version, effective product mapping, trading date, UTC state transitions, predecessor segments, status provenance and feed continuity evidence. Every unresolved minute that could change selection or admissibility needs a resolved classification or an approved exclusion. A verified no-trade minute can support a volume zero if allowed by the selected metric and policy. A cash grid that requires an observed bar still fails where a required bar is absent.
